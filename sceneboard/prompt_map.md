# Decisión de producción importante

Aunque tenemos **7 Scene Boards**, para producir bien con Seedance **yo no haría solo 7 clips finales**.

## Haría **9 módulos de video**

¿Por qué?

Porque el bloque de **solución** tiene 3 beneficios distintos y, si lo metemos todo en un solo clip, perdemos control.

## 1) AUTHORITY ANCHOR

### Función

Bloquear el Jorge definitivo del spot.

### Prompt base

```text
Create a vertical 9:16 cinematic AI commercial clip featuring Jorge Aguirre Flores as the clear authority anchor of the project.

Jorge Aguirre Flores is a mature male beauty specialist with a strong masculine, calm, professional presence. He has the same validated face, age, hairstyle, body type, and black professional uniform from the reference boards. He stands centered, calm, confident, and trustworthy. Behind him, the three problem worlds are subtly frozen as symbolic layers: time/clock, water/heat, and vanity mirror/precision. The frame must remain clean and readable.

Camera direction:
steady frontal camera, slight slow push-in, strong eye-level or subtle low-angle authority framing, no frantic movement.

Performance:
Jorge is calm, controlled, and experienced. He does not overact. He does not smile too much. He is the stable center of the film.

Visual style:
cinematic, realistic, high detail, clean professional lighting, dark elegant background with warm highlights, no text inside image.

Important:
preserve Jorge’s identity exactly. Do not change his face or age. Do not make him look like a magician, superhero, model, influencer, or doctor stereotype.

Avoid:
extra fingers, fake logos, text, exaggerated gestures, raised hands as if using powers, cluttered background.
```

## 3) HOOK ESPEJO-CAOS

### Función

Scroll stopper.

### Prompt base

```text
Create a vertical 9:16 cinematic hook clip for a fast-paced AI commercial.

A woman age 35–45 is in front of a vanity mirror during a chaotic morning. A large clock, a vibrating phone, and a few floating makeup items create controlled visual chaos. Her reflection is slightly multiplied, showing urgency and frustration. Jorge Aguirre Flores appears only as a dark partial silhouette inside the mirror reflection, not fully revealed.

Camera direction:
aggressive crash zoom into the mirror, slight lens distortion, fast visual energy, readable composition.

Performance:
the woman feels rushed and overwhelmed but still dignified. Jorge is only a mysterious silhouette presence.

Visual style:
cinematic, realistic, high detail, dark warm tones, mirror-centered composition, slightly surreal but understandable.

Important:
use only a few chaos symbols: clock, phone, makeup. Do not overcrowd the scene.

Avoid:
too many floating objects, too many reflections, grotesque expression, full reveal of Jorge’s face, fake text.
```

# Mi recomendación técnica para Seedance

## No generar con texto incorporado

Nada de:

* “30 años”
* “599 Bs”
* “WhatsApp”

Eso siempre en edición.

## Generar cada módulo con margen

Si necesitas 3 segundos útiles, genera 4–4.5 s.

## Prioridad máxima

El rostro de Jorge debe quedar bloqueado primero con:

* Authority Anchor
* CTA Anchor

Eso reduce mucho el riesgo de inconsistencia después.
